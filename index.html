Neon Slide

Cyberpunk Tile Puzzle

Slide tiles toward the gap to order them 1 to N

How to Play

  • Click or tap a tile next to the empty gap to slide it
  • Drag a tile toward the gap on touch screens
  • Use Arrow keys — the tile slides into the gap
  • Arrange tiles in order 1, 2, 3 ... N with the gap last
  • Correctly placed tiles glow gold
  • Solve in fewer moves to earn more stars
